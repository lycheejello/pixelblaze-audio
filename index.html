    1. Load patterns/reactive.js (scalar bands) or patterns/spectrum.js
    (the N-band array) on the Pixelblaze and make it the active pattern. For
    spectrum.js, its on-device array length must equal bands below.

    2. Enter its IP, hit Connect. 3. Hit Start audio and pick a tab/screen with "Share audio" checked
    (Chrome). Play music there. The strip reacts to bass/mid/treble/level
    plus the bands spectrum array.

    Offline / Spotify desktop? Set source to a virtual audio device
    (BlackHole/Loopback) instead of a tab — click list inputs, pick it, then
    Start audio. Setup: docs/offline-audio-macos.md.

    Serve over http (not https) so ws:// isn't blocked: python3 -m http.server.
