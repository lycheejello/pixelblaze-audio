strip preview — JS mirror of the device pattern (local preview only)
1. Load patterns/reactive.js (scalar bands) or patterns/spectrum.js (the N-band array) on the Pixelblaze and make it the active pattern. For spectrum.js, its on-device array length must equal bands below.
2. Enter its IP, hit Connect. 3. Hit Start audio and pick a tab/screen with "Share audio" checked (Chrome). Play music there. The strip reacts to bass/mid/treble/level plus the bands spectrum array.
Serve over http (not https) so ws:// isn't blocked: python3 -m http.server.
